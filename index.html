How to connect Google Sheets (5 minutes)
  1. On a computer, open script.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click New project.
  3. Delete everything in the editor. Then paste the code below.
  4. Near the top, find the line var SECRET = '...' and replace the text with your own long secret word. Remember it.
  5. Click the save icon. Name the project Barakah Budget.
  6. Click Deploy → New deployment.
  7. Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose Web app.
  8. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone.
  9. Click Deploy. Google asks for permission — allow it. If it warns that the app is not verified, click Advanced, then Go to Barakah Budget (unsafe). It is your own script, so this is fine.
  10. Copy the Web app URL that ends with /exec.
  11. Paste the URL and your secret word in the boxes above, then press Test.

Why "Anyone"? Your phone browser can only reach the script if it answers without a Google login screen. Your secret word is what keeps the data private — without it the script refuses every request. The spreadsheet still stays inside your own Drive. Do not share the URL or the word with anyone.
